# FormationFlow App Store Screenshots - iPad Pro 12.9" (6th Gen)

## Overview
This directory contains 10 optimized App Store screenshots for the FormationFlow cheerleading formation planning app, sized for iPad Pro 12.9-inch (6th generation) display.

**Target Resolution:** 2732×2048 pixels (Apple App Store requirement)

## Screenshot Descriptions

### 1. Formation Editor (`01_formation_editor.png`)
The main formation editor view showing athletes positioned on a court grid. Displays the interactive canvas where coaches design formations with athletes color-coded by role (Base=Blue, Flyer=Yellow, Spotter=Green, Backspot=Purple, Tumbler=Orange, Stunt Group=Pink).

### 2. Transition Player (`02_transition_player.png`)
Animation playback view showcasing smooth transitions between formations. Demonstrates the app's capability to animate athlete movements and help visualize how formations flow during routines.

### 3. Multiple Formations (`03_multi_formation.png`)
Overview showing a routine with multiple formation cards, allowing coaches to organize and view all formations in a sequence. Shows how routines are structured with multiple formations.

### 4. Roster Management (`04_roster_management.png`)
Team roster view displaying all athletes with their assigned roles and numbers. Shows color-coded role indicators and athlete details for squad management.

### 5. Inspector Panel (`05_inspector_panel.png`)
Detail inspector panel showing comprehensive information about selected athletes - name, position, role, notes, and other attributes. Demonstrates the app's detailed athlete tracking capabilities.

### 6. Court Customization (`06_court_customization.png`)
Settings interface for customizing court dimensions, grid configuration, scale, and court type. Shows options for different competition court standards (e.g., 50ft × 70ft standard cheer courts).

### 7. Transition Path Editing (`07_transition_editing.png`)
Advanced editing view for creating and visualizing transition paths. Shows side-by-side comparison of formations with movement arrows indicating how athletes move from one formation to the next.

### 8. Playback Controls (`08_playback_controls.png`)
Animation playback interface with timeline scrubber, play/pause controls, and speed adjustment. Demonstrates how coaches can preview and control routine animations frame-by-frame.

### 9. Athlete Editor (`09_athlete_editor.png`)
Form for adding or editing athlete details - name, assigned role, position on court, and notes. Color-coded role selector showing all available position types with their respective colors.

### 10. Routine Organization (`10_routine_organization.png`)
Routine structure overview listing all formations in sequence with athlete counts. Shows how coaches can organize and manage the overall routine flow and progression.
