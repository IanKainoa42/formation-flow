Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 20, 2026

Summary: FormationFlow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data stays on your device.

What data FormationFlow stores

FormationFlow stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Athlete labels (short text identifiers you create)
  • Athlete roles (base, flyer, spotter, backspot, tumbler, stunt group)
  • Formation positions on the court grid
  • Transition paths and timing between formations
  • Routine names and notes

This data is saved using your device's local storage and is never transmitted off your device.

What data FormationFlow does NOT collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No account or login credentials
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No crash reports
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No cookies or web tracking

Network usage

FormationFlow makes zero network requests. The app functions entirely offline with no internet connection required.

Third-party services

FormationFlow does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising frameworks.

Children's privacy

FormationFlow does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for use by all ages.

Data deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app. No data exists on any server to remove.

Changes to this policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
