# Paid Advertising Research for FormationFlow Launch

## 1. Apple Search Ads (ASA)

### Bidding Strategy & Budget Benchmarks for Niche Sports Apps

*   **CPT (Cost Per Tap):** $10.20 – $14.41
*   **CPA/CPI (Cost Per Acquisition/Install):** $19.74 – $26.81
*   **Conversion Rate (CR):** ~73%
*   **Tap-Through Rate (TTR):** ~7.4% – 9.7%

**Bidding Strategy:**

*   **Avoid Broad Keywords:** Bidding on generic terms like "sports" or "cheer" is expensive.
*   **Focus on Long-Tail Keywords:** Target specific, low-volume terms like "cheerleading formation ideas" or "dance team choreography app". CPTs for these can be 50-70% lower.
*   **Discovery Campaigns:** Use a "Discovery" campaign with Search Match to find new, relevant keywords directly from user searches.
*   **Custom Product Pages (CPPs):** Create custom App Store pages with visuals and messaging that match specific ad groups (e.g., a CPP for "dance coaches" with dance-specific imagery). This can increase conversion rates by 10-15%.

**Budget Recommendation:**

*   **Starting Budget:** A minimum of **$1,000–$3,000/month** is recommended to gather enough data for optimization, given the high CPTs in the sports category.
*   **Focus on ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Due to high acquisition costs, the primary success metric should be ROAS, not just a low CPI. Niche users often have higher retention and lifetime value.

## 2. Instagram & Facebook (Meta) Ads

### Targeting Cheer Coaches and Gym Owners

*   **Creative is the new targeting.** Meta's "Advantage+" algorithm is now so effective that the ad creative itself is the primary driver of audience discovery. Authentic, "lo-fi" phone footage of athletes and teams often outperforms polished ads.

*   **Core Targeting Strategies:**
    *   **"Parent" Persona (for Youth Cheer):**
        *   **Location:** 5-10 mile radius around a list of known cheer gyms.
        *   **Demographics:** Women, ages 30-50.
        *   **Interests:** Cheerleading, Gymnastics, Tumbling, Youth Sports, Competitive Cheerleading.
        *   **Parental Status:** Parents with children (Ages 6-17).
    *   **"Lookalike" Audience (Highest ROI):**
        *   Upload a Custom Audience of existing high-value users (e.g., coaches who have created multiple routines).
        *   Create a **1% Lookalike Audience** to find users with similar profiles.
    *   **"Broad" Audience (Modern Approach):**
        *   Set only **Location** (e.g., United States) and **Age**.
        *   Let the ad creative and Meta's algorithm find the right people. Use videos of impressive stunts or team successes to "call out" to the cheer community.

*   **Campaign Structure:**
    *   **Objective:** Use the **Leads** objective with **Instant Forms** to capture user information without them leaving the app. This is ideal for offering a free trial or a demo.
    *   **Offer:** Provide a low-barrier offer like a "Free Formation Template" or "Access to a Private Coaching Webinar" in exchange for their contact info.

## 3. TikTok Ads

### Reaching the Cheer Community & Ad Formats that Convert

*   **Speak the Language:** The cheer community has its own slang. Using terms like "Tic Toc" (a type of stunt) in ad copy builds authenticity.
*   **Sound is Everything:** Use high-energy cheer mixes or trending audio that matches the rhythm of tumbling and stunts.

*   **Reach & Awareness Formats:**
    *   **Branded Hashtag Challenges:** Launch a challenge like `#FormationFlowChallenge` where teams share their best formations created with the app. This drives massive User-Generated Content (UGC).
    *   **Branded Effects:** Create a filter that adds sparkle effects to a stunt or lets users "wear" a virtual team bow.
    *   **Spark Ads:** Boost existing viral videos from cheer influencers or your own profile that are already gaining traction.

*   **Conversion Formats:**
    *   **In-Feed Ads (Conversion Objective):** Use native-looking videos that feel like organic content (e.g., "Day in the Life of a Cheer Coach"). The CTA should be clear: "Download Now" or "Try for Free."
    *   **Lead Generation Ads:** Collect sign-ups for tryouts, clinics, or webinars directly within the app.
    *   **Search Ads:** Target users searching for "cheerleading apps," "stunt ideas," or "cheer competition music."

## 4. Retargeting Strategies (Post-Install on iOS)

*   **Privacy-First Approach:** With Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT), retargeting has shifted from IDFA-based tracking to behavior-based and privacy-compliant methods.

*   **Owned Channel Retargeting (High ROI):**
    *   **Push Notifications:** Re-engage users who haven't opened the app in 7 days with a message like, "Your team is waiting! Time to create a new formation."
    *   **In-App Messaging:** Target active users with prompts to upgrade to a Pro plan or try a new feature.
    *   **Email/SMS:** If you collect this data (with consent), it's the most reliable way to reach users outside the app.

*   **Paid Retargeting (SKAdNetwork & AdAttributionKit):**
    *   **SKAdNetwork (SKAN):** Use Hierarchical Conversion Values to track post-install events (e.g., "Routine Created," "Athlete Added"). SKAN 4.0 and later allows for multiple postbacks to measure long-term engagement.
    *   **AdAttributionKit (AAK):** Apple's new framework improves re-engagement tracking, helping to measure if an ad successfully brought a user back into the app.
    *   **Universal Links:** Ensure your ads and emails use Universal Links to deep-link users directly to a specific screen within FormationFlow (e.g., `formationflow://editor/new`).

*   **First-Party Data:**
    *   Encourage users to create an account early. This allows you to build a powerful first-party data asset.
    *   Use this data to create **Lookalike Audiences** on Meta and Google for new user acquisition, finding people who resemble your most engaged coaches.
