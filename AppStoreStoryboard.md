# FormationFlow — App Store Preview Storyboard
## "Fix Those Ugly Transitions" Campaign

**Objective:** Drive installs from cheer coaches who immediately recognize their own problem — embarrassing transitions — and see FormationFlow as the pro solution.

**Story Arc:** Hook (shame) → Features (tools) → Payoff (confidence)

## Key Insight
Cheer coaches are competitive. They compare themselves to other programs. The hook isn't "organize your choreography" — it's **shame + aspiration**. They've been to enough competitions to know what clean transitions look like and to be embarrassed when theirs aren't. FormationFlow gives them the tool that levels them up. The messaging should feel like it's talking to a *serious* coach, not a hobbyist.
