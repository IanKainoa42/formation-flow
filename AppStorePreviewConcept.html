Production Guide

🎨 Design System

  • Background: #0A0F1E (deep navy)
  • Accent 1: #FF4757 (coral/red — conflict, energy)
  • Accent 2: #00D4FF (electric blue — precision)
  • Gold: #FFD43B (playback, timing)
  • Font: Space Grotesk / Inter (Bold 800+)
  • Court grid: 8% white opacity lines

📐 Sizes Required

  • iPhone 6.9" → 1320 × 2868 px (primary)
  • iPhone 6.7" → 1290 × 2796 px
  • iPad Pro 13" → 2064 × 2752 px
  • Use Canva "App Store Screenshot" template or Figma at 1x → 390 × 844
  • Safe zone: keep text 60px from edges

🎬 Video Preview Tips

  • First 3 seconds are autoplay — hook must show in 3s
  • Ideal: Screen 1 text appears, then cross-fade to app demo
  • Max 30 seconds per preview (aim for 15-20s)
  • No sound required — design for muted autoplay
  • Shoot on iPad in landscape then crop to portrait
